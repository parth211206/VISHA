Progressive neurological symptoms (including eyelid drooping/ptosis, bulbar weakness, or breathing difficulty) or spontaneous systemic bleeding are emergency warning signs requiring immediate emergency clinical assessment and stabilization. The treating clinical team should follow the current approved snakebite-management protocol, including its antivenom criteria.
